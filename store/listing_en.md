## 3. Full description (≤ 4,000 chars)

```
Bake wood-fired pizza. Ride your bicycle. Photograph birds.
A fully offline pixel-art cozy game set in real regions of Korea — Pizza and Bird.

Slip a fresh pizza into your backpack, pedal through tunnels into the next town,
and fill your field guide with 598 Korean bird species, one photo at a time.

────────────────────────
HOW IT WORKS
────────────────────────
· BAKE — wood-fired pizzas (Margherita, Diavola…) in the hearth, homestyle pizzas
  (Bulgogi, Sweet Potato…) in the kitchen oven. Nail the timing minigame for a masterpiece.
· RIDE — the bicycle is the only vehicle. 11 models, custom paint, accessories.
  No cars, no scooters, no teleporting. Just you and the road across Korea.
· SHOOT — aim through a pro camera viewfinder: AF box, distance gauge, real EXIF.
  Sensor size, focal length, image stabilization — your gear decides your star rating.
  The shutter clicks, and a polaroid print slides out.

────────────────────────
v0.4.0 "FOUR SEASONS"
────────────────────────
· Seasons — in-game days turn into spring petals in Chuncheon, summer fireflies,
  autumn leaves, and winter snow at Cheorwon Plains with visiting cranes.
  Every season changes which birds appear.
· Photo Album — your shots are collected, rated, and sold. Build your own field notebook.
· Town Stories — 12 side episodes across 12 cities. A grandmother's winter guest on
  Jeju, a kid dancing with gulls in Busan… learn birding etiquette the gentle way.
· Regional Pizzas — Chuncheon dakgalbi pizza, Sokcho squid pizza, Busan fish-cake
  pizza, Jeju black-pork pizza and more. Only cookable in their home town!

────────────────────────
ALSO INSIDE
────────────────────────
· 598 bird species from the official Korean checklist — from sparrows to the
  legendary red-crowned crane and crested ibis
· 20 real birding hotspots (Cheorwon, Suncheon Bay, Upo Wetland, the DMZ…) on a
  zoomable map of the peninsula drawn from real coordinates
· Day/night cycle — streetlights glow and owls come out
· Weather — rain, snow, gusts. Snow piles on rooftops and leaves footprints
· A deep camera-gear system — compact cameras to interchangeable-lens bodies,
  with real-world specs driving the photo score
· Level up as a birder (up to Lv. 25) — titles, outfits, and 6 upgradeable skills
· Buy houses in cities you visit and decorate them. Pet the alley cat for luck
· Main story "The Map We Share" — 8 chapters finishing grandmother's old notebook

────────────────────────
A QUIET PROMISE
────────────────────────
· Fully offline — zero network connections. Works on planes and in the mountains
· Zero permissions — no location, no contacts, no storage prompts
· No data collected — your save stays on your device
· Backup codes — instead of the cloud, copy a code by hand. Your data stays yours

Take it slow. What birds will you meet today?
```

## 4. Romanization & naming rules (번역 규칙 메모)

- **지명은 국립국어원 로마자 표기법 통일**: 서울 Seoul · 인천 Incheon · 춘천 Chuncheon ·
  강릉 Gangneung · 속초 Sokcho · 대전 Daejeon · 전주 Jeonju · 대구 Daegu · 광주 Gwangju ·
  울산 Ulsan · 부산 Busan · 제주 Jeju · 철원 Cheorwon · 순천만 Suncheon Bay · 우포늪 Upo Wetland ·
  임진강/DMZ Imjin River / DMZ.
- **새 이름은 국제 영어명(eBird/ Birds of the World 관례) 우선**: 두루미 Red-crowned Crane ·
  재두루미 White-naped Crane · 흑두루미 Hooded Crane · 팔색조 Fairy Pitta · 저어새 Black-faced
  Spoonbill · 따오기 Crested Ibis · 물총새 Common Kingfisher · 황조롱이 Common Kestrel ·
  괭이갈매기 Black-tailed Gull. (게임 내 미표기라도 홍보 이미지·영문 설명에서 일관 유지)
- 음식명: 닭갈비 dakgalbi · 어묵 eomuk(fish cake) · 흑돼지 black pork — 소문자 일반명사 처리,
  필요시 괄호 병기. "Pizza and Bird" 브랜드명은 번역하지 않는다.
- 톤: 감성 문장은 직역 대신 의역. "완전 오프라인" = *fully offline* (offline-first 금지 —
  사실과 다른 업계 용어로 읽힌다).
